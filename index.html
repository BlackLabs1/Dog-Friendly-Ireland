      About this app

      Dog Friendly Ireland shows places across the island of Ireland that welcome dogs: cafés, pubs, restaurants, places to stay, shops, parks and beaches, plus everything for your dog, from vets and groomers to dog parks, kennels and rescues.

      Where the information comes from

      Places come from OpenStreetMap, the free map of the world built by volunteers. A place appears here when someone has recorded its dog policy, or when it is a service for dogs.

      For photos, reviews and up-to-date opening hours, every place has a Google Maps button that opens it in your Google Maps app.

      Add a place or fix a mistake

      Know a dog-friendly spot that's missing? Add it to OpenStreetMap and it will appear here after the next update. The easiest way on a phone is the free Every Door or StreetComplete app, or use the Fix this on OpenStreetMap link on any place.

      To mark a place as dog friendly, give it the tag dog=yes (dogs inside), dog=outside (outside only) or dog=leashed (on a lead).

      Always check first

      Dog policies change. If it matters, ring ahead, especially for accommodation, and bring a lead.
